PHONE LINK ALTERNATIVE

Phone Link copy and paste not working?

Microsoft Phone Link can share your clipboard between Windows and Android, but only on certain phones and only with the right settings. Here is how to check both, and what to use if your phone is not supported.

By Tanishk Tewetia, developer of FerryClip · Last reviewed November 12, 2026

Why Phone Link clipboard sync isn't working

The most common reason is that the phone is not supported. Microsoft's cross-device copy and paste page lists the phones that get the feature:

  • Samsung phones on One UI 2.1 or later
  • Surface Duo
  • Selected HONOR, OPPO, OnePlus, Realme, ASUS ROG, vivo and Xiaomi models with a recent Link to Windows app

If your phone is a Pixel, Motorola, Nothing or another brand that is not listed, the Cross-device copy and paste switch may be missing or may not work. No setting will change that.

If your phone is supported, check these

  1. In Phone Link on Windows, open Settings → Features and turn on Cross-device copy and paste.
  2. Update the Link to Windows app on the phone and Phone Link on the PC.
  3. If the phone is not on Wi-Fi, turn on syncing over mobile data, or connect both devices to Wi-Fi.
  4. Make sure the phone shows as connected in Phone Link, then copy text again.

Use FerryClip with any Android phone

FerryClip is a free, open-source app that syncs clipboard text between Windows and any Android 10+ phone over your local network. It does not depend on the phone brand or a Microsoft account.

  • PC → phone: copy on Windows and the text is on your unlocked phone automatically.
  • Phone → PC: copy on Android, then tap Send to PC in Quick Settings or the notification. Android requires this tap for privacy; here is why.
  • Private by design: text travels directly between your devices over TLS 1.3 with mutual authentication. There is no cloud clipboard.

Switch in two minutes

  1. Download FerryClip for Windows and the Android APK.
  2. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi or the PC's hotspot.
  3. On Windows, choose Generate code to connect. Enter the six-digit code in FerryClip on Android.
  4. Optionally turn off Phone Link's cross-device copy and paste so the two do not both write to the clipboard.

Full steps with screenshots are in the setup guide.

If your phone is supported and you want images, calls and messages too, Phone Link is the better fit. If you want clipboard text on any Android phone without your copies leaving the local network, use FerryClip.

Other options

SwiftKey syncs the clipboard through your Microsoft account if you use it as your keyboard. KDE Connect is a broader open-source phone-to-PC toolkit that includes clipboard sharing. Google Quick Share can send text one item at a time. The full guide to sharing a clipboard between Windows and Android compares all five.

Frequently asked questions

Does Phone Link clipboard work on Google Pixel?

Pixel phones are not in Microsoft's list of devices supported for cross-device copy and paste, as of November 2026. Check Microsoft's support page for changes.

Can I use FerryClip and Phone Link at the same time?

Yes, for different jobs. Phone Link can keep handling calls and messages while FerryClip handles the clipboard. Turn off Phone Link's copy and paste feature to avoid duplicate clipboard writes.

Is FerryClip free?

Yes. FerryClip is free and open source under the MIT License, with no account or subscription.
